# Netflix Titles: Exploratory Data Analysis

Exploratory data analysis of 8,807 Netflix movies and TV shows using Python, Pandas and Matplotlib.

## Objective
To understand what content Netflix offers, how its catalog grew over time, and which countries, genres and ratings dominate it.

## Dataset
Netflix Movies and TV Shows dataset from Kaggle (`netflix_titles.csv`), with 8,807 rows and 12 columns.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

## Steps Performed
1. Loaded and explored the data
2. Cleaned the data (filled missing values, converted dates)
3. Created 6 visualizations, each with a written insight
4. Summarized the key findings

## Key Findings
- About 70% of titles are movies and 30% are TV shows
- Netflix added far more titles each year from 2016, peaking around 2019
- The United States produces the most content, followed by India and the UK
- International Movies, Dramas and Comedies are the top genres
- Most content is rated TV-MA or TV-14
- The typical movie runs about 90 to 100 minutes
